CalvinX, also known as Calvin or CalvinX2, is a former Member of Parliament for the district of Deajginia, the former judge of the Magnate Courthouse, and the current Prime Minister of the Republic of Melonia. Calvin ran unopposed for the Deajginia district during the February 15, 2023 election. His campaign during that election led him to form the CalvinBall Party along with Prime Minister awedball. However, during the April 15, 2023 election, Calvin did not win re-election, ending his M.P. term.

Calvin also served as the judge presiding over the case of Republic of Melonia v. SNHK, a criminal case arising from SNHK's attempt to assassinate the Prime MInister at the time, tz_scion. The defendant, SNHK, pleaded guilty to all charges levied by the Republic, and Calvin ended up deciding his sentence.

After this landmark court case, on May 16, 2023, President Michael Webb nominated Calvin to become the full-time judge of the Magnate Courthouse on 2 Capitol Drive in Downtown. Parliament voted unanimously to confirm him as a judge. He served on the court for about a month. He then relinquished his position as Judge when accepting the nomination from President Scion to become Prime Ministe. This was done to abide by a clause in the Melonian Constitution that prevents judges from holding the position of Prime Minister.

President Scion chose Calvin to become his first Prime Minister right after his election on June 15, 2023. Parliament voted unanimously to adopt Calvin as PM.

Tenure as Member of Parliament (February 15, 2023-April 15, 2023)

Calvin co-wrote the Farm Registration Act with Speaker of Parliament tz_scion. The Farm Registration Act establishes the registration of automatic farms in Melonia and allows citizens to know exactly what farms exist in Melonia.

Tenure as Judge of the Magnate Courthouse (May 16, 2023-June 15, 2023)

Before he was confirmed as the full-time judge of the Magnate Courthouse, Calvin served as the judge in the landmark case Republic of Melonia v. SNHK, a criminal case arising from SNHK's attempt to assassinate the Prime MInister at the time, tz_scion. The defendant, SNHK, pleaded guilty to all charges levied by the Republic, and Calvin ended up deciding his sentence.

Calvin also oversaw the proceedings of CRIM-3: Republic of Melonia v. Mexican3636, where the Republic alleges 23 charges against Mexican3636 regarding arson, destroying signs, and stealing items from various chests.

Tenure as Prime Minister (June 15, 2023-present)

As Prime Minister, Calvin is spearheading “Project Walmart”, a project with the aim of constructing a public villager trading hall with 252 villagers to reduce the amount of time needed for players to get to a high level of gear. It is being constructed on 2 Park Street. There will be 18 villagers per profession, with 36 librarian villagers.

Personal Melonian Activities

Calvin is the owner of Assorted Industries, which is a business that will sell various products and acquire them through various means. He also co-founded the CalvinBall Party along with awedball.
