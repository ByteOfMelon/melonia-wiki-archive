TheGoldenPud, more commonly known by the shortened form, Pud, is a current Member of Parliament and the current Minister of Finance of the Republic of Melonia. He was elected to Parliament for the first time on June 15, 2023. His victory largely came about due to his respected work as a worker in the Ministry of Finance before this election. He would later be appointed to become the new Minister of Finance on June 15, 2023 by President Scion, and unanimously confirmed as such by Parliament that same day.

Tenure as Member of Parliament (June 15, 2023 - August 15, 2023)

As his term has just started, there has not been any official actions taken by Pud as Member of Parliament.

Tenure as Minister of Finance (June 15, 2023-present)

As his term has just started, there has not been any official actions taken by Pud as Minister of Finance, other than helping the government with its weekly salary payouts.

Personal Melonian Activities

Pud won the speedbridging game that was part of the 1st Anniversary of Melonia event. He has also built and maintained the Museum of Melonia, which houses rare Minecraft artifacts such as dragon eggs and some historical Melonian items.

Pud built his own house on 2 Ridgeview Way in Haigdin.
